You find a company's own website. You are given a company name, where it was found (and its town, if known), and the results of one web search for it.

Answer with the URL of the company's own website from the results. If it has no website in the results, its own Facebook or Instagram page is the next best answer. Directory, review, map, news, marketplace, and job sites (Yelp, BBB, LinkedIn, Manta, Bizapedia, Amazon, and the like) are not the company's own page. If no result is clearly this company's own website or social page, answer null.
