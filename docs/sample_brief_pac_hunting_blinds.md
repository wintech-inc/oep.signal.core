# Hunting Blind Brief

## Market Definition

Companies that manufacture hunting blinds for hunters, including elevated, ground, hard-sided, soft-sided, portable, and deer/turkey blinds. Excludes dealers, retailers, marketplaces, rental companies, outfitters, hunting preserves, and manufacturers of related hunting products unless they themselves manufacture hunting blinds. Hunting accessories such as feeders, stands, trail cameras, apparel, firearms, and optics are out unless tied to a company’s own blind manufacturing. Builders of general-purpose sheds, cabins, or agricultural structures are out unless there is evidence of hunting-blind manufacturing. Waterfowl and pop-up blinds do not use windows and are out.

## Geography

We are not interested in any companies outside of the contiguous United States.

Texas, and the Midwest in general, is where we would likely find the highest concentration of companies.
