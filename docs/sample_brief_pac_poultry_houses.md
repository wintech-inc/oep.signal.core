# Sample Poultry House Brief

## Market Definition

Companies that own or operate commercial poultry houses, build or deliver complete poultry houses, or make or sell products and services specifically for commercial poultry houses. Narrower than the poultry industry (feed, medicine, processing, eggs, staffing are out unless tied to the houses) and narrower than agricultural buildings (pole barns, equestrian, dairy, storage are out unless poultry houses are evidenced). Backyard and hobby coops are out. Selling, financing, insuring, or brokering poultry farms as real estate is not making, building, supplying, or operating the houses.

## Geography

We are not interested in any companies outside of the contiguous United States.
